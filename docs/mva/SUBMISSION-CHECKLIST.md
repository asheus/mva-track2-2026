## The Track 2 rubric, exact weights (confirmed 23 Oct 2026)

Previously we had inferred "Scientific Rigor and Innovation = 60% of the rubric" from
the methods-description warning. That inference was right, and here are all four:

| Criterion | Weight | What it asks | Where we answer it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Scientific Rigor** | **35%** | Is the mechanism characterisation sound, and is the candidate well-supported *by that mechanism*? | `TRACK2-REPORT.md` §2–4; `CORRECTIONS-2026-10-06.md` |
| **Potential Impact** | **25%** | If validated, how much could this contribute to understanding or diagnosing MVA — for this child *or others*? | §5; `STANDARD-OF-CARE.md` |
| **Innovation** | **25%** | A genuinely creative angle, method or tool | the falsification instruments in `brain/queries/`-style scripts; the corrections ledger |
| **Scalability** | **15%** | Could the approach apply to other individuals, diseases or larger datasets? | §3.7 and §6 |

Two things follow. **Innovation is 25%, equal to Impact** — the method and tooling carry
as much weight as the biological conclusion, which argues for leading with the
instruments that killed our own candidates rather than burying them in an appendix. And
**Scalability at 15% is the criterion a single-case report most easily loses by default**;
§3.7 and §6 exist precisely to not concede it.

## Track 1 — scored on symptoms *and* genome, and the write-up is panel-reviewed

Confirmed 23 Oct 2026, and three details change how it should be filed:

- **Rank points**: top-ranked correct = 100. **Half credit if you name only one variant
  of a heterozygous pair** — so both *BUB1B* alleles must be ranked, not just the
  truncation. We hold both.
- **F-max**: rewards pinpointing without burying the answer in noise. Secondary and
  incidental findings are explicitly welcome and **do not hurt** the automated score.
- **The methods write-up is reviewed separately by a judging panel** for scientific
  rigor and depth. Track 1 is therefore not a purely automated submission, and it is
  **eligible for the Innovation Prize** independent of placement.
- Every team scoring a full 100 is included as a **consortium author** on the hackathon
  summary paper; Track 1 has no ranked cash prizes.

## Honest status of the Track 1 answer

**Is it right?** Almost certainly, on four independent lines:

1. Allele 1 (`p.Leu737Ter`) is **ClinVar pathogenic for MVA1**, rs759242053.
2. Both alleles are **confirmed in the proband's own callset** — heterozygous, PASS,
   both PASS at high confidence (`proband/confirm_variants.py`; per-sample depth and
   allele-balance values are deliberately NOT published, and GQ is reported
   qualitatively — see PUBLICATION-AUDIT.md, "Per-sample genotype quality").
3. An **unbiased within-gene filter isolates exactly this pair**: of 14 calls in the
   *BUB1B* span, 13 PASS → 7 heterozygous → **2 in a CDS exon**, and those 2 are our
   answer with **zero extras** (`proband/track1_rediscovery.py`). No position is
   hard-coded in that filter.
4. The phenotype fits and is **specific**: rhabdomyosarcoma is inside the embryonal
   spectrum Yost 2017 assigns to biallelic *BUB1B* with severe SAC impairment, and the
   growth-restriction cluster plus parental pregnancy loss match the MVA picture.

**What is genuinely weak, and must be said in the methods write-up.** The *gene* was
never discovered by us — it was established before this analysis began, and everything
since is confirmation and mechanism work for Track 2. We built **no genome-wide
prioritisation pipeline** ranking candidates from VCF + HPO terms, which is what Track 1
actually asks for. The automated metrics (rank points, F-max) score the submitted list
and will not notice; the panel-reviewed methods write-up will, since the FAQ says it is
assessed for "scientific rigor and depth of understanding."

Two further gaps: `TRACK2-PLAN.md` cites a `track1_verification.md` that **does not
exist** in the repo, and **no secondary/incidental-findings sweep was run**, so "none"
on that form field means *not assessed*.

**RESOLVED 27 Sep 2026 — the pipeline was built and the answer is now recovered, not
asserted.** `TRACK1-REDISCOVERY.md`, run by
`apps/api/scripts/mva/track1/run_pipeline.sh`. Starting from 20,104 protein-coding genes
and 5,012,204 callset records, with **no gene named at any stage**, the cascade
(PASS → non-reference → coding CDS → recessive-compatible → VEP HIGH/MODERATE impact →
gnomAD AF < 1% → re-test biallelic) leaves **31 genes**, and information-content
phenotype ranking against the proband's HPO terms puts the causal gene **first**,
carrying exactly the submitted pair. Leave-one-out over the eight phenotype terms holds
rank 1 in **seven of eight** cases; only dropping the presenting malignancy demotes it
to rank 2.

Two silent bugs in the first draft had filtered the causal gene out entirely — a
first-match gene assignment that split the pair across a readthrough transcript, and a
backward interval scan pruned on start coordinate alone. Both are documented, because
each produced a confident wrong answer.

Still true, and stated in the write-up: this is not novel-gene discovery (the
gene–disease association is already in the public HPO file), and the pipeline sees only
coding CDS variants, so structural, deep-intronic, regulatory, repeat-expansion and
mitochondrial variation are out of scope.

**Incidental findings:** the cascade surfaces 30 other genes with rare biallelic-compatible
damaging calls, some medically actionable. They are **not named, not assessed and not
published**, and every pipeline intermediate is gitignored and blocked in the exporter.
