# The two experiments, pre-registered — and what the structure already says about one of them

**We have no wet lab, so neither experiment was run.** What follows is the part that can be
done without one, and that is better done before the bench than after: measure what the
structure predicts, and write down what every possible result would mean, so no outcome can
be read after the fact to suit the thesis.

*Producer: `scripts/mva/t1008_n1002_analysis.py`, on AlphaFold AF-O60566-F1 where this
neighbourhood is high-confidence (mean pLDDT 91.5 over 990–1015, minimum 80.4).*

## Three hypotheses, not three versions of one

| | hypothesis | predicts abundance | predicts pT1008 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | folding / buried charge — current thesis | **low** | normal |
| **B** | ubiquitination at the newly installed lysine | **low** | normal |
| **C** | lost PLK1 phosphorylation at Thr1008 | **normal** | **lost** |

Our entire candidate ranking is aimed at **A**. If **C** is right, the abundance arm is
pointed at the wrong variable.

## Thr1008 is a credible PLK1 site — the motif confirms the annotation

```
  996  F F V R I L N A N D E A T V S V L G E L  1015
                   ^1002                ^ wait — see context below
  -2 E   -1 A   0 T   +1 V
  acidic at -2: YES    hydrophobic at +1: YES   =>  matches the canonical PLK1 consensus
```

So UniProt's annotation is supported by sequence, not merely asserted. That was worth
checking before building an argument on it.

## But the geometry argues against hypothesis C

| measurement | value |
|---|---|
| 1002 Cα → 1008 Cα | **10.72 Å** |
| closest 1002 side-chain atom → any 1008 atom | **11.60 Å** |
| RSA of Thr1008 | **5.8%** |
| RSA of Asn1002 | 13.9% |

**Two things fall out, and both cut against the simple version of C.**

**1 · 11.6 Å is not contact.** D1005 and E1006 are three and four residues away *in
sequence*, which is what made the idea attractive — but sequence adjacency is not spatial
adjacency, and a side chain at 1002 does not touch Thr1008. A direct steric or
electrostatic block of the phosphosite is not supported. Electrostatics do act at distance
in a low-dielectric interior, so this weakens C rather than killing it.

**2 · Thr1008 is 94% buried.** A phosphosite at 5.8% RSA cannot be phosphorylated by PLK1
in this conformation — a kinase cannot reach it. Either the AlphaFold model does not
represent the state in which phosphorylation happens, or **the site is conformationally
gated** and becomes accessible only transiently.

That second reading produces a prediction that runs opposite to C, and it belongs in the
table rather than in a footnote: **if phosphorylation requires transient local unfolding,
a destabilising mutation at 1002 could *increase* pT1008 rather than abolish it.**

## N1002R is a clean discriminator, and the arithmetic says why

| variant | side-chain volume | change vs N | charge | ubiquitinatable |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| wild type (N) | 114.1 Å³ | — | 0 | no |
| **N1002K** (the variant) | 168.6 Å³ | **+54.5** | +1 | **yes** |
| **N1002R** (the control) | 173.4 Å³ | **+59.3** | +1 | no |
| N1002S | 89.0 Å³ | −25.1 | 0 | no |

Arginine adds **more** volume than lysine at the same charge. So under hypothesis A it
should be *at least as* destabilising — which is precisely what makes it discriminating.
R is charge-matched, bulk-matched, and cannot accept ubiquitin. If K is unstable and R is
not, the difference is not folding.

## The pre-registered reading

Written before any result exists:

| N1002K | N1002R | N1002S | pT1008 | conclusion |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| low | low | low | normal | **A** — folding / buried charge. Current thesis holds. |
| low | low | **normal** | normal | **A**, and specifically the charge, not the bulk. |
| low | **normal** | normal | normal | **B** — ubiquitination at the new lysine. |
| **normal** | normal | normal | **lost** | **C** — signalling. The abundance arm is misdirected. |
| low | low | low | **lost** | A **and** C; destabilisation costs the phosphosite too. |
| low | low | low | **elevated** | A, and T1008 is conformationally gated — destabilising opens a buried site. |
| normal | normal | normal | normal | None of the three. Look at binding or localisation. |

## What this changes today

**Hypothesis C is weakened, not killed, and it got cheaper to test.** Eleven ångströms and
a 94%-buried threonine mean the direct-interference story does not hold. C survives only
in its indirect form — that destabilising the domain perturbs a conformationally gated
site — and that form predicts *elevated* pT1008 as readily as lost, which is a far less
attractive hypothesis than the one proposed.

**Hypothesis A keeps its place at the top**, now with the specific prediction that N1002R
should be as bad or worse than N1002K.

**And the experiment list is better than it was.** The pT1008 blot was worth adding when it
looked like a direct effect; it is still worth running, but as a *third* readout rather
than the thing that could overturn everything. The cheap decisive addition is **N1002R** —
one construct, one lane, and it separates A from B outright.

**What none of this does is measure anything.** These are predictions from a model, made
before the experiments, so that the experiments mean something when someone runs them.
